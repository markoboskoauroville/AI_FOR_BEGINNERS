## Three words that must never be mixed up
- SKILL: a short written rule Queen reads before EVERY answer. Written by the teacher after a mistake. Works at once, costs nothing.
- DATASET: the saved record of Queen's answers, the teacher's yes/no with reasons, and corrected answers. Queen does NOT read it;
  it is kept for TRAINING later.
- FINE-TUNING (training): changing Queen's numbers with the dataset. It has NOT happened yet (about 100 good examples are needed).

## Who did what: only as the FACTS say. If the facts do not say who noticed or fixed something, do not say it.
- The teacher (Claude) found his own two mistakes himself, in review, before anyone used the result. Marko did not catch them.
- Do not add colours, feelings, sounds or details that are not in the facts (no 'it looked like a blur').

## Form
- No title or heading at the very top: the book prints the chapter title itself. Start with a sentence.
- Quote Marko only word for word, with '...' where words are left out, exactly as in the facts.

## Phrases that are FALSE and must never be written (the teacher found them in drafts)
- "eleven mistakes" or "eleven times the teacher said no": it was ELEVEN ROUNDS OF CORRECTIONS.
- "Queen changed the title to mixed case": she never did; a fix WOULD HAVE, and was stopped.
- "the app broke", "the link was broken", "the connection broke": nothing broke; every error was stopped before it did harm.
- "it looked blurry": not in the facts.
- The word "skill" means ONLY a written rule Queen reads before every answer. Never use "skill" for a LoRA lens, an adapter
  or anything trained: write "lens" or "adapter" ("swap lenses", never "swap skills"; "a new lens", never "a new skill").
- Do not invent Marko's future plans ("later he might try ..."): only what the facts say.
- Never write how much memory a model needs WITHOUT QLoRA (no "80 gigabytes"): that number is not in the facts.
- Never write that the model "thinks": chapter 1 says it guesses the next word. Say it "still works".
